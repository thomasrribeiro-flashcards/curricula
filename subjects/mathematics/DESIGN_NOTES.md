## Domain inventory before deck allocation

The supplied domain guide identifies proof, calculation, method choice,
representation translation, counterexamples, and checking as recurring practices.
The following inventory was established before assigning proposed decks:

- Entry mathematics: quantity, arithmetic, algebraic laws, equations, functions,
  geometry, trigonometry, data and chance, notation and elementary argument.
- Discrete and foundational mathematics: logic, sets, computability, complexity,
  counting, graphs, ordered structures, and formal verification.
- Algebra and arithmetic: linear and multilinear structures, groups, rings,
  modules, fields, number theory, representation theory, categories, homology,
  commutative algebra, and algebraic geometry.
- Space and geometry: synthetic and non-Euclidean geometry, topology, smooth
  manifolds, curvature, algebraic topology, and geometric applications.
- Analysis and change: single and multivariable calculus, real and complex
  analysis, measure, functional and harmonic analysis, approximation, ordinary
  and partial differential equations, dynamics, ergodic theory, and variation.
- Randomness and inference: probability, stochastic processes, statistical
  reasoning, inference theory, regression, design, Bayesian and time-dependent
  methods, information, and mathematical learning theory.
- Applied and computational mathematics: modeling, numerical accuracy,
  scientific computing, differential-equation discretization, optimization,
  operations research, control, inverse problems, and uncertainty.
- Interfaces and practice: physical, biological, financial, and social models;
  history, communication, research integrity, and mathematics education.

The coverage allocation in ROADMAP.md will distinguish representative included
routes from deferred specialist subfields and external professional practice.
MSC2020 is a research taxonomy, not a prerequisite sequence; CUPM and ASA are
undergraduate frameworks, not whole-field ceilings. Cambridge's current course
map is illustrative and explicitly subject to change. Course boundaries and
minimal edges in this candidate are original design judgments.
