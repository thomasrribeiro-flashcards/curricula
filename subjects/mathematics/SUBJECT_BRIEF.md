## Durable outcomes

Read definitions with exact quantifiers and domains; calculate and check;
choose methods from structure; write and repair proofs; construct examples and
counterexamples; translate verbal, symbolic, numerical, graphical, geometric
and computational representations; build and challenge models; distinguish
statistical evidence, numerical evidence and mathematical proof; reconstruct
bounded research arguments only after the necessary advanced theory.

Balanced pure/applied means each has genuine depth, not an equal-count quota.
Statistics is included in mathematics because the supplied catalog assigns
its reusable capabilities here. Programming is reused from computer science;
empirical sciences remain owned by their catalog subjects.

## Diagnostic placement and mastery evidence

Diagnose a capability with an unfamiliar problem, an explanation or
counterexample, and a change of representation. Use untimed work where needed;
speed alone is not mastery. Assess arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, proof,
linear algebra, calculus, statistics and programming separately. Record dated
evidence in the future learner record. Partial readiness triggers focused
chapter/concept repair once those curricula exist, not an invented exemption
from a whole-deck dependency. No diagnostic or pilot is authored in this run.

## Conventions and evidence policy

Use real scalars by default until complex or general fields are introduced.
State whether natural numbers include zero, whether rings have identity, field
characteristic, logarithm base, inner-product linear slot, orientation, Fourier
normalization and probability conventions when relevant. Distinguish equality,
equivalence and isomorphism; exact values, approximations and tolerances; model
uncertainty and numerical error.

Elementary calculus/inference and classical smooth PDE treatments must label
their limits. Graduate measure, weak-solution and inference theory are not
implied by undergraduate recognition. Recommended courses grant no inbound
knowledge. Every local bridge must later receive an explanation–retrieval–use
sequence and a concept ledger, not a bundle of new axioms on one card.

The dated [source register](DESIGN_NOTES.md#source-register) records Common Core
for entry scope; MAA, SIAM and ASA for undergraduate perspectives; AMS/zbMATH
for research-domain coverage; and Cambridge for advanced readiness and current
representative branches. These select scope, not verify future theorem claims.
Verify mathematical claims by authoritative texts and direct derivations at
authoring time. Recheck changing software, research claims, paper versions and
licenses then. Publicly viewable does not mean redistributable. Proposal prose
is original; source figures and exercises are not copied. The reusable domain
guide remains in staged context and is not duplicated here.

## Practice outside SRS

Require sustained problem sets, full proofs, derivations, counterexample search,
model-building reports, real-data analysis, reproducible code, error/refinement
experiments, oral explanation and reading seminars. Research routes culminate
in checked dossiers or artifact reproduction, not memorized paper summaries.
Cards maintain definitions, hypotheses, method decisions and fragile subskills;
they cannot certify these complete performances.
